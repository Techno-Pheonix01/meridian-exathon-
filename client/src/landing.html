Workflow

From request to reimbursement in eight stages

Each stage has a clear owner, and that owner is notified when it's their turn.

  1. 01

    Submit request

    Employee sends destination, dates and estimated cost.

  2. 02

    Manager review

    Approve, reject, or return with a comment.

  3. 03

    Trip approved

    The trip is cleared to go ahead.

  4. 04

    Trip completed

    Employee closes out the trip.

  5. 05

    Claim with receipts

    Expenses added and receipts uploaded.

  6. 06

    Policy validation

    Limits, dates and receipts checked automatically.

  7. 07

    Finance review

    Finance inspects the claim and approves payment.

  8. 08

    Paid

    Reference recorded and employee notified.
